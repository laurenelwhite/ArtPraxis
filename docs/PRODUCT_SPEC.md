## Promise

Upload a **reference** and receive a medium-aware lesson explaining what to observe, prepare, mix, place, and check.

## Primary flow

Authenticate → upload reference → choose medium and level → generate master and stage plates → review composition, palette, materials, and steps → save automatically.

Surfaces may change (web today; mobile and other clients later). The flow intent stays the same — see [Platform Strategy](./Product/Platform%20Strategy.md).

## MVP metrics
Upload-to-generation completion, generation success, median generation time, second-lesson rate, lesson usefulness rating, and cost per completed tutorial.

## Not in the first release
Camera-based live recognition, social feed, marketplace, native mobile app, subscriptions, and automated grading of finished artwork.
