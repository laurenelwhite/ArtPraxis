# ArtPraxis Brand and UI Source of Truth

Status: Approved  
Approved: July 25, 2026  
Authority: This guide and `artpraxis-brand-kit-final.png` are the source of truth for ArtPraxis brand and UI design.

## Brand direction

ArtPraxis is handcrafted, simple, and timeless. The visual system should feel tactile, grounded in traditional studio materials, and quietly refined rather than glossy or synthetic.

Core message: **Practice. Progress. Master.**

## Color palette

| Token | Name | Hex | Primary use |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `--color-indigo` | Indigo | `#2C3E56` | Ink, depth, primary actions, headings |
| `--color-yellow-ochre` | Yellow Ochre | `#DAA35A` | Warmth, light, progress accents |
| `--color-red-earth` | Red Earth | `#A24A3A` | Earth, vitality, expressive accents |
| `--color-warm-white` | Warm White | `#F2ECE3` | Canvas, light surfaces, backgrounds |

Use the palette with restraint. Indigo is the default structural color. Ochre and red earth are accents. Warm white replaces stark digital white.

## Typography

- Display and headings: **Fraunces**
- Body copy and UI text: **Source Sans 3**
- Use generous spacing and a calm hierarchy.
- Avoid overly bold, compressed, futuristic, or geometric typography.

## Logos

- Preserve the approved ArtPraxis wordmark proportions and spacing.
- Use the brushstroke and brush artwork as shown in the approved kit.
- Do not redraw, distort, recolor, rotate, or add effects to the logo.
- Maintain clear space around every logo variant.

## Texture and materials

Approved visual materials:

- Realistic warm handmade paper
- Natural wash and pigment
- Pencil or hand-drawn line work
- Tactile paint and studio tools

Paper should remain visible, warm, sharp, and realistic. Avoid flat white backgrounds, plastic-looking surfaces, excessive blur, artificial noise, heavy gradients, and glossy effects.

## UI principles

- Primary actions use deep indigo with warm-white text.
- Secondary actions use restrained outlines on warm paper.
- Status colors remain muted and material-inspired.
- Cards and controls should be quiet, lightly bordered, and integrated with the paper surface.
- Corners may be softly rounded, but should not feel playful or inflated.
- Icons should be simple line drawings or clean photographic material objects.
- Use whitespace and alignment to create clarity instead of decorative containers.

## Imagery

- Favor natural studio light, real tools, paint, brushes, paper, and works in progress.
- Use warm, subdued photography with tactile detail and believable shadows.
- Avoid generic stock imagery, neon colors, sterile technology aesthetics, and overprocessed scenes.

## Accessibility

- Maintain readable contrast for all text and controls.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate status.
- Preserve visible focus states and adequate touch targets.
- Texture must never reduce text legibility.

## Required workflow for future updates

Before creating or modifying any ArtPraxis screen, component, marketing asset, or product UI:

1. Review this guide and the approved brand-kit image.
2. Reuse the defined colors and typefaces.
3. Preserve the tactile paper-and-pigment character.
4. Check the result against the UI principles and accessibility requirements.
5. Treat any conflicting older asset as superseded unless it has newer explicit approval.
6. Do not change this source of truth without approval from the brand owner.
