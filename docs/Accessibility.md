## Requirements (baseline)

- [ ] Text alternatives for meaningful images; decorative images `alt=""`  
- [ ] Buttons and links have accessible names  
- [ ] Focus visible on interactive controls  
- [ ] Keyboard access to primary lesson actions  
- [ ] Do not rely on color alone for state  
- [ ] Navy on ivory contrast for body/UI text  
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion` honored for brand/lesson motion  
- [ ] Hit targets ≥44px where primary  
- [ ] Forms associate labels with inputs  

## Brand note

Logo marks may be decorative inside labeled links (`aria-label` on the link). Do not convey unique information only through the brush graphic.
