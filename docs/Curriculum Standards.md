## Structure

1. **Philosophy & learning science** (`academy/00`, `01`) ground pedagogy.  
2. **Curriculum spine** (`academy/02`) defines sequences.  
3. **Medium tracks** (`03`–`10`) specialize technique without changing the six-stage lesson spine.  
4. **Shared foundations** — color, composition, perspective, anatomy (`11`–`14`).  
5. **Production systems** — image generation, templates, critique, style (`15`–`18`).

## Rules

- Medium tracks may change materials and brush advice — **not** the canonical six stages.  
- Difficulty increases by observation demand and paint commitment, not by UI complexity.  
- Vocabulary stays consistent with Lesson Standards.  
- References cited via [Reference Library](./references/REFERENCE_LIBRARY.md).  

## Levels (draft)

| Level | Expectation |
|-------|-------------|
| Beginner | Strong guidance, lighter washes, fewer simultaneous decisions |
| Intermediate | More selective darks, edge control, limited palette discipline |
| Advanced | Interpretation emphasis, refined focal hierarchy |
