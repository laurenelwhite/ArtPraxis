## Voice

Warm. Professional. Supportive. Clear. Confident.

Never patronizing. Never corporate. Never academic. Never childish.

## Prefer

- “Preparing your next demonstration”  
- “Mixing the next wash”  
- “When you are ready, begin Value Map.”  
- “Inspiration for your final piece” (for the **master** — not “copy this exactly”)  

Use [Glossary](./GLOSSARY.md) for **reference**, **master**, **stage plate**, and process labels.

## Avoid

- “Generating…” / “Processing…” / “Rendering…” / “Building pipeline…”  
- “You should have…” guilt language  
- Exclamation spam  
- Fake urgency  

## Mechanics

- Short sentences for instructions.  
- One idea per sentence when teaching a step.  
- Professional terms with plain-language support on first use.  
- Errors: calm, specific, recoverable.
