## Learning Principles

- Teach **observation**.
- Teach **understanding**.
- Reduce intimidation.
- Increase confidence.
- Celebrate progress.
- Teach professional techniques.
- Encourage **interpretation**.
- **Never** encourage exact copying.

### Lesson implications

Every lesson should:

- Teach observation.
- Never teach copying.
- Build confidence.
- Reduce overwhelm.
- Show progression.
- Feel achievable.
- Use professional terminology.
- Never feel like school.

---

## Product Principles

ArtPraxis should feel:

- Elegant
- Calm
- Professional
- Minimal
- Intentional
- Timeless
- Premium
- Museum-quality

**Never** childish.  
**Never** gimmicky.  
**Never** cluttered.

### Screen implications

Every screen should:

- Reduce anxiety.
- Reward progress.
- Show beautiful artwork.
- Feel premium.
- Feel calm.
- Never look busy.
- Never overwhelm.

---

## UX Principles

- Reduce cognitive load.
- Keep users painting.
- One decision at a time.
- Reward progress.
- Reduce friction.
- Support exploration.

Ask of every control:

> “Would this exist if I were sitting beside an instructor in a professional atelier?”

If not, remove it.

Hierarchy (always):

1. Painting  
2. Reference  
3. Instruction  
4. Navigation  
5. System status  

---

## Image Principles

- Composition **locked** to the reference / validated master.
- Professional progression stage by stage.
- Traditional painting techniques.
- Museum-quality craft.
- Realistic paint behavior.
- **No** hallucinated composition changes.

Every generated image should:

- Be compositionally identical across the lesson.
- Teach one concept at that stage.
- Reflect professional atelier instruction.
- Never introduce random changes.
- Look like a real painting in progress.

Authoritative detail: [Image Generation Standards](../Image%20Generation%20Standards.md).

## Writing Principles

- Warm
- Professional
- Supportive
- Clear
- Confident

**Never** patronizing.  
**Never** corporate.  
**Never** academic.

Prefer instructor language:

- “Preparing your next demonstration”
- “Mixing the next wash”

Avoid machine language:

- “Generating…”
- “Processing…”
- “Rendering…”

Authoritative detail: [Writing Style Guide](../Writing%20Style%20Guide.md), [Atelier Manifesto — Loading](../design/atelier-design-manifesto.md.txt).

## Brand Principles (summary)

- Deep navy, warm ivory, brush brown — approved system only.
- Logo: wordmark + realistic brush + painted stroke; brush tip connects to the stroke.
- Never improvise logos in code.
- Visual source of truth: approved logo package under `docs/references/branding/logo/`.

See [Logo Standards](../brand/Logo%20Standards.md.txt) and [public/brand/README](../../public/brand/README.md).
