## Studio Dashboard — v0.2

### Product objective

Turn ArtPraxis from a one-session generator into a persistent creative workspace.

### User value

- Resume previous lessons.
- See progress and history.
- Start a new lesson quickly.
- Build a personal gallery over time.

### Portfolio value

Demonstrates authentication, persistence, information architecture, retention thinking, and end-to-end product design.

### Future extensions

- Search
- Favorites
- Collections
- Daily practice
- AI Coach
- Subscription entitlements
